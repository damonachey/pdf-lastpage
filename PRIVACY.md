# PDF Last Page Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

PDF Last Page opens PDFs in a built-in viewer, remembers the last page you viewed, and lists your recent PDFs in the toolbar popup.

## Data the extension handles

For each PDF you open directly in a browser tab, the extension stores the following on your device:

- The PDF's web address or local file path
- A title, taken from the PDF's metadata, the text on its first page, or its file name
- The last page you viewed

The bundled PDF.js viewer also stores view settings in your browser, such as page, zoom and scroll position, identified by a fingerprint of the file.

## How the data is used

This data is used only to show your list of PDFs and to reopen each PDF where you left off.

## Sharing

All of this data stays in your browser. The extension does not send it to the developer or to anyone else, does not sell it, and does not use it for advertising or for determining creditworthiness.

The use of information received by this extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing your data

Click the ✗ next to an entry in the popup to remove it from the list. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data.
